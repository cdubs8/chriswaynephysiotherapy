Your
questions

Everything to know before your first visit.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with us. If you are claiming through insurance, check with your provider first in case they have their own requirements.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

We often have same-day or next-day appointments available, although it depends on the day. The quickest way to see what is open is to book online or call us on (604) 703-6470.

What if the next appointment is too far away?

If the next available appointment is further away than you would like, you can join our waitlist and we will let you know if an earlier time opens up. You can also call us on (604) 703-6470.

What happens at my first appointment?

We talk through your history, examine how you move, explain what we find, start treatment and give you your first exercises. Your first visit is usually 30 or 45 minutes, or 60 minutes for TMJ physiotherapy. See more on our What to Expect page.

What is the difference between an initial visit and an initial visit - express?

The express initial visit is a shorter first appointment (30 minutes instead of 45) for patients who want to be seen sooner. Your history is spread over two sessions, but your treatment time is the same. Both options are $125.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the sore area, such as shorts or a loose top. Bring any scans or reports you have, and your insurance or claim details if they apply.

Is parking available?

Yes. There is plenty of parking at the clinic, so you can arrive without worrying about finding a spot.

How much does physiotherapy cost?

It varies by service, such as physiotherapy, TMJ physiotherapy, shockwave therapy, ICBC and pediatric care. Click here to see our updated price guidelines on the Services page, including visit lengths and what is covered.

Is physiotherapy covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans cover physiotherapy, but coverage varies, so check your annual limit and what your plan requires. ICBC physiotherapy treatments have no user fee. We are unable to provide physiotherapy for WorkSafeBC claims. If you are unsure about billing, call us on (604) 703-6470 and we will help.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. You will get an honest estimate after your first visit, and we review it as you improve.

What conditions do you treat?

Back and neck pain, shoulder, hip and knee problems, tendon injuries, sports injuries, joint pain and arthritis, jaw (TMJ) pain, headaches from the neck, rehabilitation before or after surgery, and injuries from car accidents. We also see babies and children from birth to 5 years for concerns such as torticollis. See the full list on our Conditions page.

Can you treat injuries from a car accident?

Yes. We treat injuries after a motor vehicle accident, such as whiplash, back and shoulder strains and headaches after a collision. ICBC physiotherapy treatments have no user fee. Call us if you have questions about your claim.

Will treatment be painful?

Most people find treatment comfortable. Some techniques, such as manual therapy or dry needling, can cause mild, short-lived soreness. We always explain what to expect and adjust to what feels right for you.

What is your cancellation policy?

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. Missed appointments and short-notice cancellations are charged the full fee. To change or cancel an appointment, call (604) 703-6470 or email info@optimumclinic.ca.
