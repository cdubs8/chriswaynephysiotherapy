Your
questions

Everything to know before your first visit.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with us. If you are claiming through insurance, check with your provider first in case they have their own requirements.

What happens at my first appointment?

We talk through your history, examine how you move, explain what we find, start treatment and give you your first exercises. Allow about 60 minutes.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the sore area, such as shorts or a loose top. Bring any scans or reports you have, and your insurance or claim details if they apply.

How much does physiotherapy cost?

Physiotherapy is $125 for your first visit and $95 for follow-up visits. TMJ (jaw) physiotherapy is $170 for the first visit and $120 for follow-ups, and shockwave therapy is $110 per visit. ICBC physiotherapy treatments have no user fee. These fees are from our fee list effective February 1, 2025, so please check the fees on our Services page or call us for the latest.

Is physiotherapy covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans cover physiotherapy, but coverage varies, so check your annual limit and what your plan requires. ICBC physiotherapy treatments have no user fee. We are unable to provide physiotherapy for WorkSafeBC claims. If you are unsure about billing, call us on (604) 703-6470 and we will help.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. You will get an honest estimate after your first visit, and we review it as you improve.

What conditions do you treat?

Back and neck pain, shoulder, hip and knee problems, tendon injuries, sports injuries, joint pain and arthritis, jaw (TMJ) pain, headaches from the neck, rehab before or after surgery, and injuries from car accidents. We also see babies from birth to 12 months for concerns such as torticollis.

Can you treat injuries from a car accident?

Yes. We treat injuries after a motor vehicle accident, such as whiplash, back and shoulder strains and headaches after a collision. ICBC physiotherapy treatments have no user fee. Call us if you have questions about your claim.

Will treatment be painful?

Most people find treatment comfortable. Some techniques, such as manual therapy or dry needling, can cause mild, short-lived soreness. We always explain what to expect and adjust to what feels right for you.

What is your cancellation policy?

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. Missed appointments and short-notice cancellations are charged the full fee. To change or cancel an appointment, call (604) 703-6470 or email info@optimumclinic.ca.
