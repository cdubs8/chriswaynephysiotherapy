Everything to know before your first visit.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. If you are claiming through insurance or a funding scheme, check the requirements with your provider first.

What conditions do you treat?

Back and neck pain, shoulder, hip and knee problems, sports injuries, joint pain, headaches and rehab before or after surgery.

What happens at my first appointment?

We talk through your history, examine how you move, explain what we find, start treatment and give you your first exercises. Allow 60 minutes.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. You will get an honest estimate after your first visit, and we review it as you improve.

Can physiotherapy help prevent future injuries?

Yes. We look at how you move and load your body, then build strength and mobility where it is needed.

Do you treat sports and gym injuries?

Yes. We work with runners, lifters, team-sport players and weekend athletes, and plan your return to sport with you.

Do you treat chronic pain?

Yes. We combine pain education, hands-on care and graded exercise at a pace that suits you.

Do you provide post-surgical physiotherapy?

Yes. We guide rehab before and after surgery in coordination with your surgeon.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the sore area, such as shorts or a loose top.
