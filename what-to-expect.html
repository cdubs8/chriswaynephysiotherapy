What to
expect

A clear look at your first visit, follow-ups and what happens along the way.

Before you come in

Please fill in your intake forms before your visit. After you book, they are emailed to you so you can complete them online, which we prefer. If you cannot, please arrive 10 minutes early to fill them out at the clinic. Having your forms done saves reception time and keeps your visit running smoothly. It also gives your clinician more time to prepare, so more of your appointment goes to your treatment.

What to wear

Comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the sore area, such as shorts or a loose top.

What to bring

Any scans or reports you have, and your insurance or claim details if they apply.

Parking

There is plenty of parking at the clinic.

Cancellations

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. Missed appointments and short-notice cancellations are charged the full fee.

Your first visit

Your first visit is usually 45 minutes, or 60 minutes for TMJ physiotherapy. If you would like to be seen sooner, a 30-minute express option is also available. Here is what happens.

01

Your story

We talk through your history, your symptoms, your goals and anything you have tried before.

02

Assessment

We look at how you move, have a feel of your area of injury, and perform tests like range of motion, strength, and posture, to find the problem.

03

Explanation

We simply explain what we find, talk through your treatment options and preferences, and give recommendations.

04

Treatment

Treatment starts straight away, using the techniques that suit you and your issue.

05

Your plan

You leave with your first exercises, home care advice and a clear idea of your next steps.

Initial visit - express (30 minutes). A shorter first appointment for patients who would like to be seen sooner. Your history is spread over two sessions, but your treatment time is the same. It costs the same as the standard initial visit.

Follow-up visits

How often you come in depends on your plan. We review your progress as you improve and update your goals along the way. Follow-up visits are usually 30 minutes, and each one includes:

01

Progress review

We check how you are responding and update your goals as you improve.

02

Treatment

Hands-on treatment chosen for where you are now.

03

Exercise

Your exercises are progressed, with simple self-management advice.

04

Next steps

We talk through what comes next, including how often you need to come in.

Treatment style

Our treatment is based on an integrated approach, using a combination of techniques from physiotherapy and other related health fields. Depending on the type of injury, one or more areas may be the focus. Your treatment may include:

Manual therapy

Hands-on treatment for joints, muscles and fascia to ease pain and help you move better.

Exercise therapy

Progressive, task-specific exercises matched to your body and your goals.

Modalities

Hot/cold, electromagnetic, mechanical, and specialized categories.

Education

Clear explanations and simple self-management tools, so you stay in charge of your recovery.

We bring together evidence-informed practice and individualized treatment strategies, combining manual therapy, therapeutic exercise and movement-based rehabilitation to help you reach your goals. Curious about a technique? Each one is explained in the Treatment Techniques section of our Services page.

No two patients are the same, and neither should their treatment be. That belief shapes everything we do. Your plan is chosen after your assessment, adjusted as you progress, and always explained, so you know what we are doing and why.
