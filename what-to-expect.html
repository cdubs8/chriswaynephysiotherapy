What to
expect

A clear look at your first visit, follow-ups and what happens along the way.

Before you come in

Please fill in your intake forms before your visit. After you book, they are emailed to you so you can complete them online, which we prefer. If you cannot, please arrive 10 minutes early to fill them out at the clinic. Having your forms done saves reception time and keeps your visit running smoothly. It also gives your clinician more time to prepare, so more of your appointment goes to your treatment.

What to wear

  • Clothing: choose clothes that let you move easily and give us access to the sore area.
    • Shorts for a hip, knee, ankle or foot issue
    • Tank top or sports bra for a shoulder or neck issue
    • Loose t-shirt for back or posture issues
    • Please remove jewelry if it will be on the treated area
  • Footwear: your usual running or gym shoes, or the footwear or orthotics you use for work or sport.
  • Assistive devices: crutches, a brace, a sling or a walker, if you currently use them.

What to bring

  • ID and insurance details: your extended health or private insurance card, policy number, and certificate or ID number.
  • Claim information: your ICBC claim number, if your injury is from a motor vehicle accident.
  • Medical records: X-rays, MRI or CT scan reports, specialist notes, or surgery or discharge papers related to your injury.
  • Medication list: your current prescription medications and doses.
  • Doctor's referral: bring it if you have one, although a referral is generally not required to book.

Parking

Parking is free, and there is plenty of space.

Cancellations & late arrivals

  • Notice period: we require 24 hours' notice for any change or cancellation. Please call us on (604) 703-6470 rather than emailing, as email may not be checked in time.
  • No-show fee: if you miss your appointment without notice, or cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, the full appointment fee is charged.
  • Insurance: extended health plans, ICBC and private insurance do not cover late cancellation or no-show fees, because no treatment was provided.
  • Arriving late: your session will still end at its scheduled time, so we can keep other patients on schedule.
  • Repeated cancellations or no-shows: if you frequently cancel or miss appointments, you may be asked to prepay for future appointments or may face further restrictions.
  • Exceptions: we understand that emergencies and unavoidable circumstances can arise. If you cannot attend for these reasons, please let us know as soon as possible. We review these situations case by case.

Your first visit

Your first visit is usually 45 minutes, or 60 minutes for TMJ physiotherapy. If you would like to be seen sooner, a 30-minute express option is also available. Here is what happens.

01

Your story

Every injury and every person is different. We start by listening to your story and learning how your symptoms affect your daily activities, work and lifestyle. Your goals and priorities guide everything that follows.

02

Assessment

Through a detailed physical assessment, we look at your movement, strength, mobility and the areas that concern you. This helps us find the contributing factors and what may be driving your symptoms.

03

Explanation

Understanding your condition is part of recovery. We simply explain what we find, discuss what may be contributing to your symptoms, and answer your questions. Together we talk through your treatment options and preferences, set realistic expectations and choose the best path forward.

04

Treatment

Your first treatment is tailored to your needs and what we found in your assessment. It may include hands-on therapy, targeted exercises, movement retraining and other evidence-informed approaches to reduce symptoms and restore function.

05

Your plan

Before you leave, we build a personalized plan with your next steps. You will know what to work on between appointments, what to expect moving forward, and how we will measure your progress toward your goals.

Initial visit - express (30 minutes). A shorter first appointment for patients who would like to be seen sooner. Your history is spread over two sessions, but your treatment time is the same. It costs the same as the standard initial visit.

Follow-up visits

How often you come in depends on your plan. We review your progress as you improve and update your goals along the way. Follow-up visits are usually 30 minutes, and each one includes:

01

Progress Review

We start by checking in on how you are feeling and how you have responded to treatment. We review your progress, talk about any changes in your symptoms, and adjust your goals as you improve.

02

Treatment

Your treatment keeps evolving with your needs and progress. It may include hands-on therapy, targeted techniques and movement-based approaches to help restore mobility, reduce symptoms and improve function.

03

Exercise & Self-Management

We progress your exercises as your strength, mobility and confidence improve. You will also get practical advice and strategies to manage your symptoms and keep making progress between appointments.

04

Your Next Steps

Together, we review your progress and talk about what comes next. That includes adjusting your treatment plan, setting new goals, and working out how often you may benefit from follow-up appointments.

Treatment style

Our treatment is based on an integrated approach, using a combination of techniques from physiotherapy and other related health fields. Depending on the type of injury, one or more areas may be the focus. Your treatment may include:

Manual therapy

Hands-on treatment for joints, muscles and fascia to ease pain and help you move better.

Exercise therapy

Progressive, task-specific exercises matched to your body and your goals.

Modalities

Hot/cold therapy, TENS, NMES, laser, shockwave, traction, dry needling and taping.

Education

Clear explanations and simple self-management tools, so you stay in charge of your recovery.

We bring together evidence-informed practice and individualized treatment strategies, combining manual therapy, therapeutic exercise and movement-based rehabilitation to help you reach your goals. Curious about a technique? Each one is explained in the Treatment Techniques section of our Services page.

No two patients are the same, and neither should their treatment be. That belief shapes everything we do. Your plan is chosen after your assessment, adjusted as you progress, and always explained, so you know what we are doing and why.
