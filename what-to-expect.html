Treatment style

Our treatment is based on an integrated approach, using a combination of techniques from physiotherapy and other related health fields. Depending on the type of injury, one or more areas may be the focus. Your treatment may include one or all of the following:

Manual therapy

Hands-on treatment for joints, muscles and fascia to ease pain and help you move better.

Exercise therapy

Progressive, task-specific exercises matched to your body and your goals.

Therapeutic modalities

Hot and cold therapy, laser, shockwave, TENS and EMS, used when they suit your injury.

Education

Clear explanations and simple self-management tools, so you stay in charge of your recovery.

Our approach combines evidence-informed practice with individualized treatment strategies to help you achieve their goals.

No two patients are the same, and neither should their treatment be. What treatment you receive is chosen after your assessment with respect to your preferences, and you will always know what we are doing and why. A treatment plan with recommendations is customized to you prioritizing your goals and concerns.

Your first visit

Your first visit is usually 45 minutes, or 60 minutes for TMJ physiotherapy. If you would like to be seen sooner, a 30-minute express option is also available. Here is what happens.

01

Your story

We talk through your history, your symptoms, your goals and your previous experiences.

02

Assessment

We look at how you move, the plus strength, flexibility and anything that may be adding to the problem, to find the root cause.

03

Explanation

We explain what we find in plain language, what might be contributing to your injury, and talk through your treatment options and recommendations.

04

Treatment

Treatment starts straight away, using the techniques that suit you and your issue.

05

Your plan

You leave with home care advice, your first exercises, and a clear idea of your next steps.

Initial visit - express (30 minutes). A shorter first appointment for patients who would like to be seen sooner. Your history is spread over two sessions, but your treatment time is the same. It costs the same as the standard initial visit.

Follow-up visits

How often you come in depends on your plan. It is never set in stone. We review your progress as you improve and update your goals along the way. Follow-up visits are usually 30 minutes and generally include:

  • A review of your progress
  • Hands-on treatment
  • Exercise progression and self-management advice
  • A look at what comes next

Before you come in
