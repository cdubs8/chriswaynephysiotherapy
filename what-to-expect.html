What to
expect

A clear look at your first visit, follow-ups and what happens along the way.

Treatment style

We combine hands-on treatment with targeted exercise and education. What you receive is chosen after your assessment, so it fits you, your goals and your body, and is based on evidence. You will always know what we are doing and why.

Hands-on care

Manual therapy to ease pain and stiffness and help you move better.

Active rehabilitation

Exercises matched to your body and progressed at every visit.

Education

Clear explanations and simple self-management tools, so you stay in charge of your recovery.

Your first visit

Your first visit is usually 30 or 45 minutes, or 60 minutes for TMJ physiotherapy. Here is what happens.

01

Your story

We talk through your history, your symptoms, your goals and anything you have tried before.

02

Assessment

We look at how you move, plus strength, flexibility and anything that may be adding to the problem, to find the root cause.

03

Explanation

We explain what we find in plain language and talk through your options.

04

Treatment

Treatment starts straight away, using the techniques that suit you and your issue.

05

Your plan

You leave with your first exercises, home care advice and a clear idea of your next steps.

Follow-up visits

How often you come in depends on your plan. It is never set in stone. We review your progress as you improve and update your goals along the way. Follow-up visits are usually 30 minutes and generally include:

  • A review of your progress
  • Hands-on treatment
  • Exercise progression and self-management advice
  • A look at what comes next

Before you come in

What to wear

Comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the sore area, such as shorts or a loose top.

What to bring

Any scans or reports you have, and your insurance or claim details if they apply.

Parking

There is plenty of parking at the clinic.

Cancellations

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. Missed appointments and short-notice cancellations are charged the full fee.
