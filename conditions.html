Conditions
we treat

Pain, injury and movement problems we help with every day.

Whatever brought you in, we start by finding the cause, not just the sore spot. Here are the conditions we see most often, grouped by area of the body. Don't see yours? Many other injuries and pain problems can be helped too, so call or book an assessment and we will talk it through.

Neck & head

  • Neck pain & postureStiffness and aching from desk work, screens and long periods in one position.
  • Wry neckA sudden, painful neck spasm that makes it hard to turn your head.
  • Headaches & migrainesHeadaches linked to neck stiffness or tension, which physiotherapy can often help.
  • TMJJaw pain, clicking or locking, treated with the Goralsky Craniomandibular Method.
  • Cervical radiculopathyAn irritated nerve in the neck that can send pain, tingling or weakness down the arm.
  • Thoracic outlet syndromePressure on nerves or blood vessels near the collarbone, causing arm and hand symptoms.
  • Upper trap painTight, achy muscles between the neck and the shoulder.
  • VertigoDizziness or spinning that can come from the neck or the inner ear.

Shoulder & arm

  • Rotator cuffPain or weakness when lifting the arm, from irritated or injured shoulder tendons.
  • Frozen shoulderA stiff, painful shoulder that gradually loses movement.
  • Tennis elbowPain on the outer elbow from overloaded forearm tendons.
  • Golfer's elbowPain on the inner elbow from overloaded forearm tendons.
  • Carpal tunnelNumbness, tingling or pain in the hand from pressure on a nerve at the wrist.
  • De Quervain'sPain on the thumb side of the wrist, common with repetitive gripping or lifting a baby.
  • ECU tendinopathyPain on the little-finger side of the wrist from overloaded tendons.
  • Scaphoid fractureA small wrist bone fracture, often after a fall onto an outstretched hand.
  • Nerve tensionPain, tingling or tightness that travels along an irritated nerve.

Spine & back

  • SciaticaPain, tingling or numbness that travels from the lower back down the leg.
  • Disc bulgeIrritation from a bulging spinal disc, causing back pain or symptoms into the arm or leg.
  • Facet jointsSharp or achy pain from the small joints of the spine, often with bending or twisting.
  • Muscle tightnessStiff, tight back muscles that limit how freely you can move.
  • Rib painPain around the ribs and mid back that can hurt with breathing, twisting or lifting.
  • Pregnancy painBack and pelvic pain during pregnancy, with gentle ways to ease it and stay active.

Hip & thigh

  • Femoroacetabular impingement (FAI)A pinching feeling at the front of the hip, often when squatting or sitting for long periods.
  • Labral tearAn injury to the cartilage rim of the hip that can cause catching, clicking or deep groin pain.
  • SIJ painPain at the back of the pelvis, often on one side, that can spread into the buttock or thigh.
  • Hip bursitisTenderness on the outer hip that makes lying on your side and climbing stairs uncomfortable.
  • Hamstring strainA pulled muscle at the back of the thigh, often from sprinting or sudden stretching.

Knee

  • ACL injuryA torn or sprained ligament that leaves the knee unstable. Rehab helps with or without surgery.
  • Meniscus tearDamage to the cartilage cushion of the knee, causing catching, locking or pain when twisting.
  • MCL / LCL injuriesSprains of the ligaments on the inside or outside of the knee.
  • Patellar tendinopathyPain below the kneecap from repeated jumping or loading, often called jumper's knee.
  • Runner's kneePain around or behind the kneecap that flares with running, stairs or long periods of sitting.
  • Prepatellar bursitisSwelling and tenderness at the front of the kneecap, often from kneeling.

Foot, ankle & lower leg

  • Ankle sprainStretched or torn ligaments that cause swelling, pain and a wobbly ankle.
  • Achilles tendinopathyPain and stiffness in the heel cord, often worse with running, jumping or the first steps of the day.
  • Plantar fasciitisHeel and arch pain, often worst with the first steps in the morning.
  • Calf strainA pulled calf muscle that hurts when you push off, run or rise onto your toes.
  • Shin splintsAching along the shin from overload, common when running is increased too quickly.
  • FHL tendinopathyIrritation of a deep tendon behind the ankle, common in dancers and runners.
  • Ankle fractureA broken ankle bone. Rehab restores movement, strength and balance once it has healed.

Accidents, surgery & children

  • Car accident injuriesWhiplash, back and shoulder strains and headaches after a collision. ICBC physiotherapy treatments have no user fee.
  • Post-surgery rehabGuided rehab before and after knee or hip replacement, ACL reconstruction, shoulder and spinal surgery.
  • Infant torticollisA head tilt or turning preference in babies, treated with gentle hands-on care and simple guidance for home.
  • PlagiocephalyFlat spots on a baby's head, with positioning and movement guidance for parents.
  • Delayed motor milestonesSupport when a baby or young child is slow to roll, sit or crawl.
