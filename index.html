Questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. If you're claiming through insurance or a funding scheme, check the requirements with your provider first.

What happens at my first appointment?

We talk through your history, examine how you move, explain what we find, start treatment and give you your first exercises. Allow 60 minutes.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the problem. We'll give you an honest estimate after your first visit and review it as you improve.

Do you treat sports and gym injuries?

Yes. We work with runners, lifters, team-sport players and weekend athletes, and build a return-to-sport plan with you.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the sore area, such as shorts or a loose top.
