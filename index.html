Questions

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly with us. If you are claiming through insurance, check with your provider first in case they have their own requirements.

What happens at my first appointment?

We talk through your history, examine how you move, explain what we find, start treatment and give you your first exercises. Your first visit is usually 30 to 45 minutes, or 60 minutes for TMJ physiotherapy.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear comfortable clothing that lets us see and move the sore area, such as shorts or a loose top. Bring any scans or reports you have, and your insurance or claim details if they apply.

How much does physiotherapy cost?

It varies by service, such as physiotherapy, TMJ physiotherapy, shockwave therapy, ICBC and pediatric care. Click here to see our updated price guidelines on the Services page, including visit lengths and what is covered.

Is physiotherapy covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans cover physiotherapy, but coverage varies, so check your annual limit and what your plan requires. ICBC physiotherapy treatments have no user fee. We are unable to provide physiotherapy for WorkSafeBC claims. If you are unsure about billing, call us on (604) 703-6470 and we will help.
