Our
services

One-on-one care built around what you need to get back to.

Physiotherapy

We find the cause of your pain, not just the sore spot then treat it with hands-on techniques and targeted exercise. Hands-on treatments include: muscle therapy (trigger point/myofascial release massage, scraping, cupping) and joint therapy (mobilisation, manipulation, traction, instrument assisted).(

  • Back and neck pain
  • Shoulder problems
  • Hip and knee pain
  • Tendon injuries
  • Joint pain and arthritis
  • Headaches from the neck

Sports and gym rehab

Train through recovery, not around it. We build a plan that gets you back to your sport stronger and less likely to be injured again.

  • Sprains and strains
  • ACL and ligament injuries
  • Running injuries
  • Lifting injuries
  • Return-to-sport testing
  • Injury prevention

Surgery rehab

Guided rehab before and after your operation so you recover faster and move with confidence.

  • Pre-surgery strengthening
  • Knee and hip replacement
  • Shoulder surgery
  • ACL reconstruction
  • Spinal surgery recovery

Dry Needling (IMS)

Invasive treatment by inserting thin, acupuncture needles into muscle knots to relieve tension.

  • Neck & Back Pain
  • Headaches/Migraines
  • TMD
  • Shoulder & Rotator Cuff
  • Tendonopathies
  • Arthritis

Shockwave Therapy

Non-invasive treatment using sound waves to stimulate the body's natural healing processes.

  • Plantar Fascitis
  • Achilles and Patellar Tendinopathy
  • Tennis/golfer's Elbow
  • Rotator Cuff and Shoulder
  • Bursitis (Shoulder, hip, knee)
  • Shin Splints

Low Level Laser Therapy

Non-invasive treatment using light energy to stimulate healing and reduce pain.

  • New muscle, ligament, and tendon injuries
  • Chronic Pain
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Neck Pain
  • Back Pain
