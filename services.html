Treatment Techniques

Your physiotherapist chooses the techniques that suit you after your assessment, so your treatment is based on what your body needs.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on treatment to relieve pain and improve movement.

Good for: muscle tightness, stiff joints and pain that limits how you move.

Book now
  • Trigger point and myofascial release
  • Scraping and cupping
  • Joint mobilisation and manipulation
  • Neurodynamic therapies
  • Traction
  • Instrument-assisted therapy

Dry Needling (IMS)

Needle-based treatment using thin acupuncture needles inserted into tight muscle knots to release tension.

Good for: stubborn muscle knots and pain that hasn't settled with other treatment.

Book now
  • Neck and back pain
  • Headaches and migraines
  • TMD
  • Shoulder and rotator cuff
  • Tendinopathies
  • Arthritis

Shockwave Therapy

Non-invasive treatment using sound waves to stimulate the body's natural healing processes.

Good for: long-standing tendon, bone, and ligament problems that have been slow to heal.

Fees: Initial visit (45 minutes) $125 · Follow-up visits (30 minutes) $110

Book now
  • Plantar fasciitis
  • Achilles and patellar tendinopathy
  • Tennis and golfer's elbow
  • Rotator cuff and shoulder
  • Bursitis (shoulder, hip, knee)
  • Shin splints
  • Calcifications

Low Level Laser Therapy

Non-invasive treatment using light energy to stimulate healing and reduce pain.

Good for: new injuries and ongoing pain when you want a gentle, non-invasive option.

Fees: Sessions are 30-60 minutes · Call to book

Book now
  • New muscle, ligament, and tendon injuries
  • Chronic pain
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Neck pain
  • Back pain
