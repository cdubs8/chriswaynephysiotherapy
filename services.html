Our
services

One-on-one care built around what you need to get back to.

Physiotherapy

We find the cause of your pain, not just the sore spot, then treat it with hands-on techniques and targeted exercise.

  • Back and neck pain
  • Shoulder problems
  • Hip and knee pain
  • Tendon injuries
  • Joint pain and arthritis
  • Headaches from the neck

Sports and gym rehab

Train through recovery, not around it. We build a plan that gets you back to your sport stronger and less likely to be injured again.

  • Sprains and strains
  • ACL and ligament injuries
  • Running injuries
  • Lifting injuries
  • Return-to-sport testing
  • Injury prevention

Massage

Remedial massage to ease tension, improve movement and speed up recovery between sessions.

  • Remedial massage
  • Deep tissue work
  • Trigger point release
  • Pre-event preparation
  • Post-event recovery

Surgery rehab

Guided rehab before and after your operation so you recover faster and move with confidence.

  • Pre-surgery strengthening
  • Knee and hip replacement
  • Shoulder surgery
  • ACL reconstruction
  • Spinal surgery recovery

Chronic pain

Persistent pain needs patience and a clear explanation. We build strength and confidence at a pace that suits you.

  • Long-term back pain
  • Persistent neck pain
  • Widespread aches
  • Pain education
  • Graded activity plans
