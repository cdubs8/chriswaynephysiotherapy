Meet Chris

Photo

Chris

Doctor of Physiotherapy, Master of Science in Rehabilitation, Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology

Chris built his practice based on how physiotherapy should be: hands-on treatment, evidence-based treatment, reactive and preventative care. After watching other clinics approach injuries with cookie-cutter approaches, Chris wanted something different. Individualized treatments tailored to your body.

Chris graduated with a Doctor of Physiotherapy degree from the University of Melbourne in Australia. Prior to this, Chris was involved in researching and publishing rehabilitation guidelines while completing his Master of Science degree and studying human movement during his Bachelor of Science degree in Kinesiology in Canada.

He values empowering his clients to actively manage their conditions by using education, manual therapy and rehabilitative exercises. Chris continues to develop professionally having trained with GLA:D Australia, Neurokinetic Therapy, Functional Movement Systems, Clinical Pilates and Neuromuscular Dry Needling.

Outside of work, Chris enjoys staying fit through various workout and sporting activities, travelling and reading self-help books.

Our Clinical Approach

Our clinical approach is built on clarity, structure and measurable progression.

Every client begins with a detailed assessment to understand the root cause of your injury rather than just your symptoms. Other areas to consider are any contributing factors, movement patterns, load tolerance, and lifestyle factors that impact your recovery.

From there, we develop an individualised plan that is tailored to your body. Treatment is individualized, adjusted, progressed in every session.

Our main focus is on active rehabilitation that is supported by hands-on treatment. We prioritize long-term resilience, recovery, and prevention over short-term symptom fixes.

Clear communication is key. Clients are educated on their injury, the rationale behind their treatment plan, and what is required to achieve long-term outcomes. This ensures each client is actively involved in their recovery and not just a passive recipient of care.
